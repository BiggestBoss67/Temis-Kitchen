About Temi's Kitchen

Temi's Kitchen is a small food business located in Table View, Cape Town. The business offers Nigerian and West African cuisine, providing customers with traditional flavours and a selection of food options.


Our Story

Temi's Kitchen brings Nigerian and West African food to the Cape Town community. Its menu includes traditional dishes and popular favourites, offering customers an opportunity to experience authentic flavours from the region.


Our Purpose

Our Mission

The proposed mission of Temi's Kitchen is to provide customers with enjoyable Nigerian and West African food while creating a welcoming experience for people who appreciate traditional cuisine.


Our Vision

The proposed vision is to become a recognised food destination in Cape Town for customers seeking authentic Nigerian and West African flavours.


Our Values

  • Authenticity: Celebrating traditional Nigerian and West African flavours.
  • Quality: Providing enjoyable and carefully prepared food.
  • Customer Experience: Creating a welcoming experience for customers.
  • Community: Making West African cuisine accessible to the local community.
