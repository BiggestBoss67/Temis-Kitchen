About Temi's Kitchen

Temi's Kitchen offers authentic Nigerian and West African cuisine, providing customers with rich traditional flavours across Cape Town.

Our Story

Founded with a passion for West African culinary culture, Temi's Kitchen brings community and heritage together through carefully crafted dishes.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To deliver rich, authentic West African meals while offering a welcoming experience for all food lovers.

Our Vision

To become Cape Town's premier destination for authentic West African culinary experiences.

Our Core Values

  • Authenticity: Preserving traditional West African recipes.
  • Quality: Using fresh ingredients and authentic spices.
  • Community: Celebrating food culture and customer relationships.
